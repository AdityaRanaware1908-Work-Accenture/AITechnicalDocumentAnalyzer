REQ-001: The system shall authenticate users using multifactor authentication (MFA) for all online banking sessions.

REQ-002: The system shall complete 99% of fund transfers within 2 seconds under 1,000 concurrent users.

REQ-003: The system shall prevent duplicate debits and credits when a transfer request is retried by the user.

REQ-004: Every successful fund transfer shall debit the source account and credit the destination account exactly once.

REQ-005: The system shall recover from a regional outage with a recovery time objective (RTO) of 30 minutes and a recovery point objective (RPO) of 5 minutes.

REQ-006: All Transaction audit records shall be retained for 10 years and protected against unauthorized modification.

REQ-007: All data transmitted between application components shall be encrypted in transit.
