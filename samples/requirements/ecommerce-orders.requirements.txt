REQ-001: The system shall send an order confirmation email to the customer within 60 seconds of a successful payment.

REQ-002: The system shall apply free shipping to all orders with a subtotal of $50 or more.

REQ-003: The checkout page shall load quickly and support a large number of shoppers.

REQ-004: Managers and finance staff who have completed security training shall be able to approve refunds.

REQ-005: Payment transaction records shall be retained for the audit retention period.

REQ-006: Customers shall be allowed to cancel an order after payment confirmation, provided the order has not yet been dispatched.

REQ-007: Customers shall not be allowed to cancel an order after payment confirmation, even if the order has not yet been dispatched.

REQ-008: The order service shall never reject a valid order.

REQ-009: The product search shall return results within 1.5 seconds for 98% of searches under 2,000 concurrent shoppers.

REQ-010: Customer payment card numbers shall be stored in encrypted form.
